Preventive Maintenance
On (default): a PM waits until its work order is completed or cancelled before generating another. The due date is not changed while it waits — the PM reads as overdue and generates as soon as you close the old one. Applies to fixed and floating schedules alike.
Off: a PM raises its next work order on schedule whether or not the last was ever touched, so several can pile up against one job.
Floating schedules only.
On: canceling a floating schedule workorder triggers the setting of the new due date the same as completing it.
Off (default): canceling a floating schedule pm keeps the PMs next due date unchanged.
